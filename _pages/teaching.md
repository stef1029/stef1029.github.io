## Open Science in Practice

**[Cambridge Open Lab](https://cambridgeopenlab.org/) summer school — Homerton College, University of Cambridge,
3–7 August 2026.**

I taught engineering techniques for neuroscience on this week-long school,
running practical workshops for PhD students in electronics design with Arduino
and in CAD with FreeCAD — both chosen because they are free, open, and still
usable once the week is over and everyone has gone back to their own lab.

The premise is one my own work has made me believe in. A great deal of
experimental neuroscience is bottlenecked not on ideas but on instrumentation,
and a researcher who can design a circuit board or a printed part is far less
constrained in the questions they can ask. Nearly everything on my
[portfolio]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) page exists because building it
was cheaper, faster, or simply more capable than anything available to buy.
